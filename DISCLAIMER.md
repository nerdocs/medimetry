# Disclaimer

medimetry is a software library that reproduces clinical formulas and scoring systems exactly as published in the
scientific literature cited in each function's docstring. It is provided for software developers and researchers.

## Not a medical device

medimetry is not a medical device within the meaning of Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR) or any comparable
legislation. It has no medical intended purpose. It is not intended to be used, alone or in combination, for the
diagnosis, prevention, monitoring, prediction, prognosis, treatment or alleviation of disease, injury or
disability, nor for providing information used to take decisions with diagnostic or therapeutic purposes.

The library returns numeric results and the categories defined by the cited publications. It does not interpret
results, does not issue recommendations and does not replace clinical judgement.

## Responsibility of integrators

Anyone who incorporates medimetry into a product that has a medical intended purpose becomes the manufacturer of
that product and is solely responsible for its qualification, classification, risk management, verification,
validation and regulatory compliance. In such a product medimetry has to be treated as software of unknown
provenance (SOUP) under IEC 62304 and validated accordingly.
